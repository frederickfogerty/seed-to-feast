# Garden Genius

Build me an app to take food preferences, amount of space available, location, availability of greenhouse, and experience and produce: list of crops and quantity of seeds required

instructions for bed creation and preparation (orientation, fertility requirements, tools needed, and time estimate, etc.)

a planting plan for spring, summer, and autumn (and winter, if there is a greenhouse)

simple instructions on a week, by week basis

what and when to harvest

and so on
